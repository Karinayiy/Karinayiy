### 🔬 Research

**From a gaokao ellipse to the onset of chaos: how the caustics of elliptic billiards break** · *independent paper, 2026*  
Starting from Problem 16 of the 2025 Chinese national college entrance exam, I prove the caustic theorem and the invariance of d₁d₂ with high-school tools, then deform the ellipse into a family of tables. Chaos appears for every nonzero deformation, and the chaotic fraction grows linearly. 11 pages, 31 automated checks, 9M bounces per table.  
[📄 Paper (PDF)](https://karinayiy.github.io/assets/papers/elliptic-billiards.pdf)

**Linear theory, numerics, and curvature in the Gray–Scott model** · *independent paper + interactive app, 2026*  
A reproduction and extension of Pearson (1993). Only 0.35% of the parameter plane is Turing-unstable, and none of the 11 pattern types lie there. Includes a von Neumann stability analysis and simulations on spheres and tori.  
[🧪 Interactive app](https://karinayiy.github.io/gray-scott/) · [📄 Paper (PDF)](https://karinayiy.github.io/assets/papers/gray-scott.pdf)

**A Hardy–Berndt-type sum** · *research assistant to my mentor Zimeng Ye, 2026*  
Analytic number theory: divisor formulas, prime denominators, and the growth of T₂(c). An interactive lab computes the sums exactly and checks the theorems numerically, e.g. 29, 37 and 3373 are the only primes below 10⁶ for which S(p) is an integer.  
[🧮 Interactive lab](https://karinayiy.github.io/hardy-berndt/)

**Stock Signal Lab: can public data predict a stock's next week?** · *data science, 2026*  
18 look-ahead-free features, walk-forward models from 2016 to 2026, ~21,800 out-of-sample predictions. Direction is not predictable (AUC 0.49), but volatility is (R² = 0.41 vs 0.23 naive), and volatility-based sizing cut the worst drawdown from −53% to −43%.  
[📈 Live dashboard](https://karinayiy.github.io/stock-signal-lab/)
